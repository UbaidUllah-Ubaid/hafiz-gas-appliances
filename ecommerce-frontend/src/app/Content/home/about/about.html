WHO WE ARE

Our Story

More Than Products, We Build With Purpose.

At Hafiz Gas Appliances, we believe that quality begins with the people who make the product. That is why we manufacture our own tandoors instead of simply selling products made by others.

From selecting materials to shaping and assembling each unit, we focus on careful workmanship and practical design. Our goal is to deliver gas appliances that customers can rely on for their everyday cooking needs.

We take pride in our work and continuously aim to improve the quality and experience we offer to our customers.

MADE WITH CARE

We Don't Just Sell Tandoors. We Build Them Ourselves.

Our tandoors are manufactured by our own team. We pay attention to construction, material selection and finishing to create products designed for practical use.

Being directly involved in manufacturing helps us maintain better control over the work and focus on the quality of every product we make.
